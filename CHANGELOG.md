# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-26

First release.

- Fits sphere and capsule PhysBone colliders to skinned and static meshes.
- Humanoid presets (Light, Standard, Detailed), anatomical axes and left/right mirroring.
- Non-humanoid support with per-part modes and volume-based splitting.
- Skips PhysBone-driven bones automatically and routes unmerged outfit armatures to the avatar's bones.
- Scene view preview, performance rank readout, PhysBone assignment and re-linking on regenerate.
- Works with any VRChat SDK that includes PhysBones, with no compile-time dependency. Unity 2019.4+.
